# Lesson 1: The Path from Need to Checked Result

## Assessment Answers

### Easy

#### 1. In simple words, what is the task path?

The task path is a series of seven steps that help us use an AI assistant to complete a task effectively. It starts with understanding what we want to achieve and who the result is for, then providing useful information and asking the AI to do a specific task. Finally, we make our own decisions and check the result before trusting it.

#### 2. Why should a student decide the purpose and user before asking an AI tool for help?

A student should decide the purpose and user first so the AI understands what needs to be done and who the result is meant for. This helps the AI provide a more relevant and useful answer instead of giving general information that may not meet the student's needs.

#### 3. Name the two parts of the task path that always stay with the person.

The two parts that always stay with the person are:

- **Human decision:** Deciding between options based on personal needs and judgment.
- **Human check:** Checking important facts and making sure the final result is correct and suitable.

### Intermediate

#### 4. The Naruto club has the hall only from 4 PM to 8:30 PM. Why should this detail be in the AI request?

The hall timing is an important constraint because the entire event must take place between 4 PM and 8:30 PM. Including this information helps the AI create a realistic schedule that fits the available time and avoids activities running beyond the hall's closing time.

#### 5. The assistant gives an event plan ending at 8:25 PM. What should the club still check before sharing the plan?

The club should check whether all activities, including judging, prize distribution, and closing arrangements, can realistically finish by 8:25 PM. They should also confirm that the schedule leaves enough time to complete the event before the strict 8:30 PM deadline. The organisers must review the plan and make sure it works in practice before sharing it.

### Hard

#### 6. A student wants help making a revision timetable. Write the seven task-path answers in short points: purpose, user, useful input, AI task, expected result, human decision, and one check.

**1. Purpose:** To create a revision timetable that helps me prepare effectively for my upcoming exams.

**2. User:** The timetable is for me, based on my subjects, study needs, and available time.

**3. Useful Input:** My subjects, exam dates, syllabus, difficult topics, available study hours, and other daily commitments.

**4. AI Task:** Create a realistic revision timetable that distributes the subjects and topics across my available study time.

**5. Expected Result:** A clear, organised timetable with dates, study times, subjects, topics, and breaks.

**6. Human Decision:** I will decide whether the timetable suits my learning pace, priorities, and daily routine, and adjust it if necessary.

**7. One Check:** I will check that all important topics are included and that the timetable fits my actual available time before following it.

#### 7. Two friends are arguing about who should apologise first. Does the task path fit here? Explain why or why not.

The task path does not fully fit this situation because the main issue is a personal disagreement that requires communication and mutual understanding. An AI assistant can help the friends think about the situation or find respectful ways to communicate, but it cannot make the final decision about who should apologise first. The friends need to discuss the issue and decide together.
